**Pulsify v0.11.0 — Notifications & Real-Time Activity**
Stay aware of everything happening on your server as it happens. This release adds a real-time activity system that keeps the dashboard live without a single page refresh.

**Live activity feed**
- **Real-time updates** — member joins and leaves, moderation actions, role changes, event activity, channel updates and triggered automations all appear the moment they occur.
- **Detailed entries** — each item carries a timestamp and a detailed view, so you can see exactly what happened and who was involved.

**Stay organized**
- **Categories and filtering** — narrow the feed to just the kinds of activity you care about.
- **Read and unread states** — with unread badges so nothing important slips past.

**On your terms**
- **In-app toasts** — optional pop-up notifications when new activity arrives.
- **Per-type preferences** — choose precisely which events should notify you, and which stay quiet.
- **Automatic cleanup** — older notifications are retired on their own, keeping the feed relevant.

The result is faster awareness, quicker moderation response, and a dashboard that always reflects your server's current state.
