**Pulsify v0.6.0 — Moderation Panel**
A complete moderation workspace inside Pulsify, so your team can manage members and respond to issues without ever leaving the dashboard.

**Full moderation toolkit**
- **Member actions** — timeout, remove timeout, kick, ban, unban, warn, change nickname, and add or remove roles.
- **Message management** — delete an individual message or bulk-delete many at once.
- **Ban list** — review every ban and lift them with a single click.

**Safe and accountable**
- **A reason on every action** — record why each action was taken, for a clear paper trail.
- **Moderation logs** — every action is saved to a complete, reviewable history.
- **Confirmation dialogs** — destructive actions ask for confirmation before they run.
- **Permission and hierarchy checks** — Pulsify verifies both your access and the bot's role position before acting, and explains clearly when an action would be rejected by Discord.

Every action is mirrored on Discord instantly, keeping the dashboard and your server perfectly in sync.
