**Pulsify v0.1.0 — Project Initialization**
The first Pulsify release establishes the foundation the entire platform is built on. There are no user-facing features yet — this groundwork is what makes everything in the releases that follow possible.

**What's included**
- **Web dashboard** — a new Next.js application (TypeScript, App Router) that will host the entire Pulsify experience.
- **Pulse bot** — the Discord bot that powers Pulsify is connected and online, ready to act on your server.
- **Sign in with Discord** — secure authentication through Discord OAuth, so the servers you manage and your permissions are recognized automatically.
- **Database and infrastructure** — a managed Supabase backend for authentication and data storage, plus the project structure and environments needed for fast, reliable development.

With the platform's core wired together end to end, development of Pulsify's features begins from here.
