# Pulsify v0.9.0 — Channels & Server Structure

Build and reorganize your entire server layout from a single screen, without the friction of managing it inside Discord.

**A complete view of your server**
- **Structure tree** — see every category and channel at once, including text, voice, stage and forum channels.

**Full channel management**
- **Create** channels and categories quickly.
- **Edit settings** — name, topic, NSFW flag, slowmode, and bitrate and user limits for voice channels.
- **Reorganize** — drag and drop to reorder, and move channels between categories.
- **Duplicate and delete** channels as your server evolves.

**Fine-grained access control**
- **Permission overwrites** — manage per-role and per-user permissions on any channel.

**Insight**
- **Channel statistics** — message activity, active users and voice usage per channel.

Changes are validated against the bot's permissions and applied to Discord immediately, so reorganizing even a busy server stays fast and safe.
