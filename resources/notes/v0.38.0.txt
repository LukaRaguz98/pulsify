**Pulsify v0.38.0 — Management Analytics**
Statistics shows what's happening and Insights shows what to do — Management Analytics shows who's doing it. A dedicated Analytics view for the people running your community: their moderation, support and community work, all in one place, with rankings, response times and accountability built in.

**What's new**
- **Analytics › Management** — a new view focused entirely on staff performance and management effectiveness, using the same 24h / 7d / 30d / all-time date ranges as the rest of your analytics so the period is consistent everywhere.
- **Management overview** — active staff, moderation actions, support actions and community actions at a glance, each with a period-over-period trend.

**Staff performance**
- **Moderation breakdown** — warnings, timeouts, kicks, bans, unbans and total actions tracked per moderator, with performance rankings.
- **Support analytics** — tickets handled and resolved, average first-response and resolution times, resolution rate and support workload distribution.
- **Community management** — announcements published, events created, giveaways managed and more, attributed to the staff behind them.

**Rankings & leaderboards**
- **Know your top contributors** — most active moderator, support member and administrator, fastest responder and most tickets resolved, all filterable by timeframe.
- **Drill-down profiles** — click any staff member for a full breakdown of their moderation, support and community activity, active days and consistency.

**Smart management insights**
- **Workload balancing** — Pulsify highlights overloaded staff, support bottlenecks, exceptional contributors and members who've gone quiet, with recommendations to rebalance the load.

**Built for real teams**
- **Visualizations** — a stacked activity-over-time chart and a contribution breakdown make the split between moderation, support and community easy to read.
- **Search, filter & export** — search staff, filter by role and export the full performance breakdown to CSV. Responsive, polished and consistent with the rest of Pulsify.

Run your community with the visibility, accountability and recognition your team deserves.
