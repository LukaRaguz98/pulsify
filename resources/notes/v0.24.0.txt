**Pulsify v0.24.0 — Giveaways & Community Engagement**
Keep your community buzzing with built-in giveaways — create them on the dashboard, run them in Discord, and let Pulse handle entries and winners for you.

** What's new**
- **Giveaways view** — a brand-new section under Engagement showing your active, scheduled and ended giveaways with entry counts, winners and prizes at a glance.
- **Create in seconds** — set a title, prize, duration, winner count and channel, then preview the exact Discord embed before you publish. Start from a template (Quick drop, Nitro, Members-only, Milestone) to skip the setup.
- **Fully automatic** — Pulse posts the giveaway with a live countdown and a Join button, tracks every entrant, draws winners when the timer ends, and announces them — no manual work.
- **Schedule ahead** — line up a giveaway to start later, and it goes live on its own.
- **Reroll & end early** — draw winners on demand or reroll a finished giveaway in one click.

**Fair play, built in**
- **Entry requirements** — gate entry by role, minimum account age, time in the server, or message activity.
- **Anti-alt & blacklist** — block throwaway accounts and bar specific users from entering.

**Insights & history**
- **Engagement analytics** — participation rates, most active giveaways, entry trends and completion rate.
- **Export entrants** — download the full participant list as CSV any time.

Find **Giveaways** in the sidebar under Engagement — responsive, animated, and updating live. 💜
