**Pulsify v0.26.0 — Levels & XP**
Your members now level up. Pulsify tracks activity as XP, turns it into levels, and rewards your most active and trusted community members — built right into the Members view alongside the reputation system you already know.

**Earning XP**
- **Activity earns XP** — members gain XP for sending messages, hanging out in voice, using commands, entering giveaways, and showing interest in events.
- **Fair by default** — a per-message cooldown and anti-spam protection keep things honest, and you can ignore specific channels or roles.
- **Your curve, your pace** — tune exactly how much XP each activity gives and how steep the climb to the next level is.

**Rewards & level-ups**
- **Reward roles** — automatically grant a role when a member hits a level. Keep every role earned, or only the highest.
- **Level-up messages** — celebrate level-ups in the channel they were active in, a dedicated channel, or a private DM, with a customizable message.

**See who's on top**
- **Levels in the directory** — every member now shows their level and XP, and you can sort by either.
- **Leaderboards** — a new Leaderboard tab ranks members by level, XP, reputation and recent activity, with timeframe filtering and level analytics.
- **Profile progression** — each profile gets a Level & XP card with an animated progress bar to the next level.

**In Discord**
- **/rank** — check your level, XP, progress and server rank (or look up another member).
- **/leaderboard** — show the top members by XP, with your own standing.

Find it all under **Members**, and tune it from **Level settings**.
