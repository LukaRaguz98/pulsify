**Pulsify v0.54.0 — Temporary Roles**
A new Temporary Roles tab in Server > Roles for granting time-limited access and rewards — Pulse removes the role automatically when it expires, and logs every step.

**Assign with an expiry**
- **Any duration you need** — grant a role for minutes, hours, days, weeks or months, or pick a custom expiration date and time.
- **Start from a preset** — VIP for 30 days, Event Access for 24 hours, Giveaway Winner for 7 days or Trial Moderator for 14 days, then tweak from there.

**Automatic expiration**
- **Hands-off cleanup** — a background sweep removes expired roles from Discord, marks them expired and logs the event, so nothing lingers past its time.
- **The member stays informed** — an optional Pulse v2 DM lets them know when a role is removed, plus a heads-up when a grant is within 24 hours of expiring.

**Built to integrate**
- **Every grant is attributed** — each one records where it came from: Manual, Economy, Marketplace, Giveaway, Event, Automation, Application, Moderation or Other.
- **Rewards across Pulsify line up** — automated grants from anywhere in the dashboard are tracked the same way, so credit always lands in the right place.

**Manage at a glance**
- **Live monitoring** — see active grants, what's expiring soon, what recently expired, the most-assigned roles and a 14-day assignment trend.
- **Full control** — extend, shorten, remove or bulk-remove grants, with search and filters by status and source, and countdown badges that tick down in real time.

**Audited and in sync**
- **An append-only audit log** — assigned, extended, shortened, expired and removed events are recorded with the actor, target, timestamp and source.
- **Stays in step with Discord** — role changes synchronize back to the server and degrade gracefully around deleted roles, departed members and permission limits.
