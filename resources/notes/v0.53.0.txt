**Pulsify v0.53.0 — Server Assets Manager**
A dedicated Server › Assets page that puts your emojis, stickers and soundboard sounds in one place — with bulk tools, drag-and-drop uploads and one-click exports that beat Discord's native settings.

**Everything in one view**
- **Server › Assets** — separate tabs for Emojis, Stickers and Soundboard, with at-a-glance totals, animated-emoji counts and live slot-usage bars for every category, driven by your boost tier.

**Manage emojis, stickers & sounds**
- **Browse your way** — search, filter and sort, switch between grid and list views, and open any asset for a full preview, including in-dashboard playback for soundboard sounds.
- **Edit in place** — rename, delete or duplicate emojis, and export any single asset straight to your computer.

**Import without leaving Pulsify**
- **Drag-and-drop uploads** — browse or drop one or many files at once, with type and size validation, duplicate-name detection and rename-before-upload, so Pulse pushes each one straight to Discord.

**Bulk tools**
- **Act on many at once** — select multiple assets to rename, export or delete them together, with confirmation prompts on anything destructive.

**Export anything**
- **From one to everything** — export a single asset, your whole selection, an entire category, or a full server asset package, bundled as a ZIP with a metadata manifest of names and types.

**Safe by design**
- **Clear when something's off** — plain errors when slots are full, file formats are invalid or Pulse is missing the Manage Expressions permission, and every change syncs straight back to Discord.
