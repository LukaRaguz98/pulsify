# Pulsify v0.4.0 — AI Server Setup Assistant

Pulsify's first AI-powered feature helps administrators set up a polished, professional server in seconds. Describe your community, and Pulse generates the content for you.

**What it generates**
- **Welcome message** — a warm, on-brand greeting for new members.
- **Server rules** — a clear, ready-to-post set of guidelines.
- **Onboarding instructions** — guidance that helps newcomers find their way around.
- **Channel and category suggestions** — a sensible starting structure for your server.

**Built for control**
- **Tone selection** — choose Professional, Gaming, Community, Minimal or Friendly, and the output adapts to match your community's character.
- **Preview before applying** — review everything before anything touches your server.
- **Regenerate freely** — not quite right? Generate again until it fits.
- **Apply to Discord** — push the generated welcome message straight to your server in one step.
- **Fair usage** — generation requests are rate-limited to keep the feature fast and dependable for everyone.

A standout shortcut that turns a blank server into a finished community in minutes.
