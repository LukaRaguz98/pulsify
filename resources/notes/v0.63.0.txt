**Pulsify v0.63.0 — Server History**
Something changed and nobody remembers changing it. Until now that meant digging through Discord's Audit Log, guessing at dates, and cross-referencing half a dozen dashboard pages. Pulsify now keeps the whole story in one place: every significant change to your server, in order, whether it was made in the dashboard, through a slash command, or directly in Discord.

**Analytics History**
- **One chronological record** of roles, channels, members, moderation, economy, automations, events and server configuration.
- **Grouped by day** and colour-coded by category, so a month of history is something you can actually skim.
- **Every card tells you who** made the change — and, uniquely, **where from**: the dashboard, the Discord client, a slash command, or Pulse acting on its own.

**See exactly what changed**
- Expand any event for the **previous value and the new value**, field by field — a role rename, a permission grant, a verification level, a settings edit.
- Affected members, related modules and the raw metadata are all there too, with one click through to the page the change belongs to.

**Find the one you're looking for**
- **Filter** by category, administrator, member, module, event type or date range.
- **Search** across members, roles, channels, keywords and administrators.
- Click an administrator or an affected member inside an event to instantly narrow the whole history around them.

**Export it**
- **CSV** for spreadsheets, **JSON** for tooling, and a formatted **PDF report** for sharing.
- Export your current selection, the filtered view, or the complete history.

**Know your server's rhythm**
- Events today and this week, your **busiest periods** and busiest day, your **most active administrators** and the **most modified modules** — all at the top of the page.

**In Discord too**
- **`/timeline`** shows the most recent changes without leaving Discord, filterable by category or member.

**A tidier sidebar**
- The dashboard navigation has been regrouped around what you're actually working on: **Members**, **Content**, **Economy**, **Safety**, **Server**, **Pulse Bot** and **Analytics**.
- People-shaped pages now sit together under Members — the directory, roles, onboarding, invites, milestones and birthdays — while everything you publish into the server lives under Content.
- Nothing moved out of reach and no links broke. The staff performance page is now called **Staff**.

**Plans**
- History is included on **every plan, Free included**. How far back it reaches follows your plan's history retention — 30 days on Free, up to a year on Enterprise. Nothing is ever deleted, so upgrading brings older history straight back.
