**Pulsify v0.37.1 — Onboarding polish & integration fixes**
A focused follow-up to v0.37.0: a more modern welcome embed, a faithful editor preview, and a fix for duplicated integration notifications.

**Onboarding**
- **Modernised welcome embed** — cleaner community links, action buttons (verify, finish and links) on a single row, and a tidier overall layout.
- **Preview matches reality** — the editor preview now mirrors exactly what gets posted to Discord.
- **Custom banner** — drop in your own banner image instead of the generated one.
- **General community channels** — highlight general chat, introductions, music, bot commands, support and announcements.

**Quick setup**
- The server overview now opens a **Quick setup** that configures your server in minutes and saves it as an editable template.

**Integrations**
- **No more duplicate notifications** — an update is now delivered exactly once, even across restarts or multiple bot instances.
- Editing a connection's source re-baselines it instead of backfilling old activity.
