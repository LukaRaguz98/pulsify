**Pulsify v0.51.0 — Community Polls & Voting**
Run polls from the dashboard and let members vote right inside Discord — every vote tracked, tallied and analysed in Pulsify.

**Make any kind of poll**
- **Engagement › Polls** — single choice, multiple choice, yes/no, ratings, or feature voting. Pick a template to start fast, or build your own.
- **Anonymous polls** keep voter names private while still tracking the result, and you can let voters change their mind or lock votes as final.

**Vote in Discord**
- Pulse posts a polished poll embed with vote **buttons** (small polls) or a **select menu** (bigger or multi-pick polls), showing live vote + voter counts and a live countdown.
- Votes are recorded instantly; single-choice replaces a prior vote, multiple choice lets members pick up to your limit.

**Schedule + auto-close**
- Publish immediately or schedule for later, and set how long a poll runs — Pulse closes it on time, tallies the result and announces the outcome.

**Decide who can vote**
- Gate voting by **role**, **account age**, **minimum level** or **minimum reputation**.

**Community governance**
- Optional **weighted voting** (by reputation or level), **approval thresholds** and **minimum participation** so a result only "passes" when it truly has community backing.

**Track everything**
- **Engagement › Poll Results** — closed-poll history, vote breakdowns, participation and engagement trends.
- Live results, countdowns and CSV export on every poll's detail view.
