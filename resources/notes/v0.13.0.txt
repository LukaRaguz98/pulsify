**Pulsify v0.13.0 — Quality of Life Improvements**
A focused release that polishes the experience across the bot and dashboard, with particular emphasis on consistency, communication and customization.

**Better moderation communication**
- **Warning DMs** — when Pulse Guard warns a member, they now receive a direct message explaining it, styled to match Pulsify's moderation embeds so the experience feels consistent.

**A refreshed /sync command**
- **Modern embed** — the `/sync` response has been redesigned into a cleaner, branded embed that reflects your configured Pulse Guard color.

**Per-server bot branding**
- **Custom identity** — give the Pulse bot a custom display name and avatar on each server, so it fits right into your community.
- **Safe and previewable** — uploaded images are validated for type, size and dimensions, with a preview before saving and a one-click reset to default branding.
- **Audited** — branding changes are recorded in your activity feed.

**Smaller refinements**
- Confirmation modals for sensitive changes, copy-to-clipboard buttons, clearer empty states, unsaved-changes warnings, loading skeletons, and more consistent success feedback throughout the dashboard.

Dozens of small improvements that together make Pulsify feel noticeably more refined.
