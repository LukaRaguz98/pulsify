PULSIFY-32 — Mobile Optimization, QA Testing & Stability Update (v0.32.0)

Description
	Implement a comprehensive mobile optimization and quality assurance update focused on making the entire Pulsify platform fully responsive across all devices while identifying, testing, and resolving bugs throughout the application.
	This update should ensure that every view, component, dialog, form, and workflow works seamlessly on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices while improving overall platform stability and user experience.

Goals
	Achieve full mobile responsiveness across the platform
	Improve usability on phones and tablets
	Identify and fix application bugs
	Improve platform stability and polish
	Prepare Pulsify for public adoption and scaling
