**Pulsify v0.32.1 — More Mobile Polish**
A quick follow-up to v0.32.0 with another round of mobile refinements so the whole dashboard feels right on a phone.

**What's new**
- **Tables reflow into cards** — on phones, the moderation, member, role and log tables now stack into clean labelled cards, so every detail is readable without scrolling sideways or squashing columns. The Channels list was tidied up to fit too.
- **Charts go landscape** — a narrow chart now prompts you to rotate (or tap to open) a full-screen landscape view, giving graphs real room to breathe instead of being crammed into a column.
- **No more sideways drift** — pages can no longer be dragged left and right on mobile; the whole app stays locked to your screen width.
- **Action buttons that wrap** — the buttons in the top-right of each view now stack neatly under the title on phones instead of running off the edge and getting cut off.

**Also**
- **Tidier top bar** — notifications and the live latency chip now sit cleanly in the mobile top bar instead of floating over your content.
- **Server picker tune-up** — the Workspaces button collapses to a single icon on phones to save space, with a little breathing room added around your profile.
- **Landing page fixes** — removed an unnecessary black strip on the right edge, brought the menu fully into view, and fixed sections that stayed blank while scrolling on mobile.

Pulsify just keeps getting comfier on the small screen.
