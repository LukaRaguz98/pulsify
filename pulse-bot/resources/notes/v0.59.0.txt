**Pulsify v0.59.0 — Alt Risk Detection**
A new Alt Detection view in Safety scores any account against the alt-account signals Pulse can see, surfaces the accounts that may be related, and gives your team one place to investigate — so ban evasion and throwaway accounts stop being a guessing game.

**Account lookup**
- **Search any account** — by username, @mention or Discord ID, including members who already left or were banned.
- **The whole picture** — avatar, display name, ID, account creation date, server join date, roles, current status, activity and moderation history, in one report.
- **An Alt Risk Score** — 0-100, banded Low, Moderate, High or Critical, with every factor that moved it listed and weighted.

**Signals, not guesses**
- **Twelve inputs** — account age, join recency, default avatar, activity, moderation history, reputation, economy footprint, giveaway entries, applications, onboarding, verification and prior safety flags.
- **Evidence in its favour, too** — an established account, a long activity history or a trusted reputation pull the score back down; the report shows both sides.
- **Tuned to be useful** — a legitimate newcomer lands in Moderate, not Critical: signals that need time (activity, onboarding) stay quiet until a member has had a fair chance.

**Potential linked accounts**
- **Correlated, never confirmed** — similar usernames, near-simultaneous joins, accounts created together, shared moderation history, coin transfers between accounts and matching activity hours.
- **A confidence percentage** — each potential match shows how strong the evidence is and exactly which indicators fired. Automatic confidence is capped below certain on purpose: Discord exposes no IP or device data, so nothing here proves an alt.
- **Your call, recorded** — link two accounts yourself when you're sure, and the link becomes the one thing Pulse treats as certain.

**Investigations**
- **A real queue** — Pulse scores every account as it joins and opens an investigation for the high and critical ones, so the suspicious joins of the past week are already waiting for you.
- **Case files** — statuses (Open, Monitoring, Cleared, Confirmed alt, Banned), moderator notes, outcomes and a timeline of every decision your team made.
- **Linked account groups** — accounts linked indirectly (A to B, B to C) are collected into one group.

**In Discord**
- **/alt-check** — a moderator command that returns the account's age, risk score, risk factors, potential linked accounts and a recommendation, in a Pulse embed. Every check is recorded in the dashboard's lookup history.

Judgement stays with your moderators — Pulse just makes sure they have the evidence in front of them.
