**Pulsify v0.42.0 — Server Recovery & Backup System**
The safety net for your server: versioned snapshots of your configuration that you can review, compare and restore in a few clicks — so an accidental change, a moderation mistake or a misconfiguration is never permanent.

**A dedicated Backup Center**
- **All your backups in one place** — a new Backup & Restore page lists every snapshot with its name, type, version, size and creation date.
- **Search & filter** — find any backup fast and filter by how it was made (manual, daily or weekly).
- **At-a-glance status** — see your total backups, last backup, storage used and whether automatic backups are on.

**Automatic backups**
- **Set it and forget it** — schedule **daily** or **weekly** backups and let Pulse capture them for you, no clicks required.
- **Retention you control** — keep the most recent N automatic backups; older ones are pruned automatically so storage stays tidy.
- **Choose what's captured** — pick exactly which sections each automatic backup includes.

**Captures everything that matters**
- Roles, channels & categories (with permissions), automations (welcome/goodbye/auto-role), moderation routing, member onboarding, Pulse Guard, tickets, and a record of giveaways, events and announcements.

**Preview before you restore**
- **See the changes first** — every restore shows exactly what will be created, what will be overwritten, and what exists on your server but isn't in the backup.
- **Confirm with confidence** — nothing is applied until you review the preview and confirm.

**Selective, safe restores**
- **Restore only what you need** — bring back individual sections (just roles, just channels, just onboarding…) instead of the whole server.
- **Additive & safe by default** — restore creates missing roles and channels and rewrites configuration without touching anything else.
- **Full revert when you want it** — opt in to "Remove roles & channels not in this backup" for a true point-in-time revert that deletes everything added since (with a clear warning, since deleting a channel removes its messages).

**Compare any two backups**
- **Visual change summary** — pick two snapshots and see what was added, removed or modified between them, grouped by section.

**Share & clone servers**
- **Download any backup** as a portable JSON file to keep it safe off-platform or hand it to someone else.
- **Import a shared backup** — drop in a file (or paste JSON) someone sent you, then restore it to spin up a copy of their server's configuration on yours.

**Recovery logs**
- **Full audit trail** — every backup, restore, deletion and schedule change is recorded with who did it, when, and whether it succeeded.

**Polished throughout**
- Status indicators, clear restore warnings, loading/error/success states and a fully responsive layout.

Backup & Restore is part of the Pro toolkit. Manage your community with confidence.
