**Pulsify v0.54.0 — Temporary Roles**
A new Temporary Roles tab in Server > Roles for granting time-limited access and rewards — Pulse removes the role automatically when it expires, and logs every step.

**Assign with an expiry**
- Grant any role for minutes, hours, days, weeks or months, or pick a custom expiration date. Start from a preset — VIP for 30 days, Event Access for 24 hours, Giveaway Winner for 7 days, Trial Moderator for 14 days.

**Automatic expiration**
- A background sweep removes expired roles from Discord, marks them expired, logs the event and (optionally) DMs the member with a Pulse v2 notice — plus a heads-up when a grant is within 24 hours of expiring.

**Built to integrate**
- Every grant records where it came from — Manual, Economy, Marketplace, Giveaway, Event, Automation, Application, Moderation or Other — so automated rewards from across Pulsify are attributed correctly.

**Manage at a glance**
- Live monitoring of active grants, what's expiring soon, what recently expired, the most-assigned roles and a 14-day assignment trend.
- Extend, shorten, remove or bulk-remove grants, with search and filters by status and source — and countdown badges that tick down in real time.

**Audited and in sync**
- An append-only audit log records assigned, extended, shortened, expired and removed events with the actor, target, timestamp and source. Role changes synchronize with Discord and degrade gracefully around deleted roles, departed members and permission limits.
