**Pulsify v0.44.0 — Contextual Help & Interactive Guidance System**
Learning Pulsify just got easier. Little information icons now sit next to the things that matter — page titles, cards and advanced options — and a quick hover or tap explains what a feature does, when to use it, and what to watch out for. No more leaving the dashboard to figure out how something works.

**Guidance, right where you need it**
- **Info on every view** — across the whole dashboard and workspaces: Overview, Moderation, Pulse Guard, Analytics, Insights, Events, Giveaways, Tickets, Onboarding & Verification, Members & Reputation, Levels & XP, Automations, Scheduled, Announcements, Backup & Restore, Billing, Integrations, Templates, Presence, Roles, Channels, Commands, every settings page, and all workspace tools.
- **Concise, useful copy** — each tip leads with what the feature does, then when to use it, then a practical tip, heads-up or best practice.
- **Always shows the plan** — every tip ends with the tier it needs (**Free**, **Plus** or **Pro**) in your accent colour, so there are no surprises about what&apos;s included.

**Works the way you do**
- **Hover or tap** — tips open on hover and keyboard focus on desktop, and on tap/click for touch devices.
- **Never in the way** — tooltips float above the page, auto-flip to stay on-screen, follow the page as you scroll, and dismiss on Escape, outside click or pointer-away.
- **Readable everywhere** — content stays legible and well-sized on small screens.
- **Polished & accessible** — a subtle fade-in (that respects reduced-motion), proper labels and roles, and full visual consistency with the Pulsify design language.

**Your dashboard, your call**
- **Show Contextual Help** — a new toggle under Preferences → Guidance lets seasoned users switch the info markers off across the whole app (and back on any time).
- **Remembered for you** — your choice persists across sessions.
