**Pulsify v0.8.0 — Events Management**
Plan, run and review Discord events entirely from Pulsify, with a calendar, cover images and real participation insight.

**Manage every event**
- **Create, edit, cancel and delete** events without opening Discord.
- **All event types** — Voice channel, Stage and External events are fully supported.
- **Cover images** — add artwork and preview the event before publishing it.

**See your schedule clearly**
- **Calendar view** — visualize everything on a proper calendar.
- **Organized sections** — upcoming, live and past events, each grouped for quick scanning.
- **Search and filtering** — find any event in seconds.
- **Recurring events** — set up events that repeat automatically on a schedule.

**Understand attendance**
- **Participant lists** — see exactly who is interested in each event.
- **Event analytics** — track participation and attendance trends, and identify your most popular events.

Every change syncs with Discord, so your dashboard and your server always show the same schedule.
