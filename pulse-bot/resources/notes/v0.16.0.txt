**Pulsify v0.16.0 — Member Profiles & Reputation**
This release helps you truly understand your members through detailed profiles, activity insight and a reputation system built for community administration.

**Detailed member profiles**
- **The complete picture** — avatar, display name, join date, roles, activity statistics, voice activity, message count and moderation history, all on a single page.

**Reputation system**
- **Trust scoring** — a reputation score calculated from activity, moderation history, account age and overall engagement, surfaced with clear indicators and badges.

**Activity tracking**
- **What members do** — messages sent, voice time, command usage, active channels and event participation.
- **History and heatmaps** — an activity timeline and visual heatmaps reveal patterns at a glance.

**Moderation insight**
- **Full history** — complete with risk indicators and repeat-offender detection.
- **Moderator-only notes** — private comments your team can leave on a member, never shown to the member.

**Find anyone fast**
- **Advanced search and filters** — active, inactive, high-risk, new members and staff.
- **Quick actions** — warn, timeout, ban or assign roles right from the profile.

A complete view of every member, designed to make moderation and community management far easier.
