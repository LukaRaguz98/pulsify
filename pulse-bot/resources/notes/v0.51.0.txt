**Pulsify v0.51.0 — Community Polls & Voting**
Run polls from the dashboard and let members vote right inside Discord — every vote tracked, tallied and analysed in Pulsify.

**Make any kind of poll**
- **Engagement › Polls** — single choice, multiple choice, yes/no, ratings, or feature voting. Pick a template to start fast, or build your own.
- **Anonymous polls** — keep voter names private while still tracking the result, and let voters change their mind or lock votes as final.

**Vote in Discord**
- **Buttons or a menu** — Pulse posts a polished poll embed with vote buttons (small polls) or a select menu (bigger or multi-pick polls), showing live vote and voter counts and a live countdown.
- **Instant, accurate tallies** — votes are recorded instantly; single-choice replaces a prior vote, multiple choice lets members pick up to your limit.

**Schedule + auto-close**
- **Set it and forget it** — publish immediately or schedule for later, and set how long a poll runs; Pulse closes it on time, tallies the result and announces the outcome.

**Decide who can vote**
- **Four ways to gate** — restrict voting by role, account age, minimum level or minimum reputation.

**Community governance**
- **Make results count** — optional weighted voting (by reputation or level), approval thresholds and minimum participation, so a result only "passes" when it truly has community backing.

**Track everything**
- **Engagement › Poll Results** — closed-poll history, vote breakdowns, participation and engagement trends.
- **Drill into any poll** — live results, countdowns and CSV export on every poll's detail view.
