**Pulsify v0.53.0 — Server Assets Manager**
A dedicated Server › Assets page that puts your emojis, stickers and soundboard sounds in one place — with bulk tools, drag-and-drop uploads and one-click exports that beat Discord's native settings.

**Everything in one view**
- **Server › Assets** — separate tabs for Emojis, Stickers and Soundboard, with at-a-glance totals, animated-emoji counts and live slot-usage bars for every category (driven by your boost tier).

**Manage emojis, stickers & sounds**
- Search, filter and sort, switch between grid and list views, and open any asset for a full preview — including in-dashboard playback for soundboard sounds.
- Rename, delete, duplicate emojis, and export any single asset straight to your computer.

**Import without leaving Pulsify**
- Drag-and-drop or browse to upload one or many files at once, with type and size validation, duplicate-name detection and rename-before-upload — Pulse pushes each one straight to Discord.

**Bulk tools**
- Select multiple assets to rename, export or delete them together, with confirmation prompts on anything destructive.

**Export anything**
- Export a single asset, your whole selection, an entire category, or a full server asset package — bundled as a ZIP with a metadata manifest of names and types.

**Safe by design**
- Clear errors when slots are full, file formats are invalid or Pulse is missing the Manage Expressions permission — and every change syncs straight back to Discord.
