**Pulsify v0.19.0 — Legal, Support & Community Pages**
This release completes Pulsify's public pages, bringing the transparency, support resources and professionalism expected of a finished product.

**Legal**
- **Privacy Policy** — a clear explanation of what data Pulsify collects, how Discord OAuth data is used, analytics and cookies, data storage and security, and the third-party services involved.
- **Terms of Service** — acceptable use, prohibited behavior, service limitations, moderation and automation disclaimers, account termination, liability and intellectual property.

**Support**
- A dedicated Support page with contact options, support categories, a troubleshooting guide, and clear paths for reporting bugs and requesting features.

**Community**
- A Community page featuring the Discord invite, a changelog, a roadmap preview, ways to contribute, and social links.

**FAQ**
- A dedicated, categorized FAQ page answering the most common questions about Pulsify and the Pulse bot.

**Throughout**
- Consistent layouts, SEO metadata, smooth page transitions, full mobile responsiveness, and footer links that route correctly across the site.

Everything a new or prospective user needs to understand, trust and get help with Pulsify.
