**Pulsify v0.5.0 — Analytics & Statistics**
This release gives administrators a genuine understanding of their community through a redesigned Overview and a dedicated Statistics tab full of meaningful metrics.

**A redesigned Overview**
- **Activity charts** — visualize messages over time, member joins and leaves, voice activity, command usage and moderation activity.
- **Engagement at a glance** — quickly see your most active channels and most active members.

**The new Statistics tab**
- **Detailed metrics** — total messages sent, voice channel time, executed bot commands, moderation actions and active users.
- **Activity patterns** — peak activity times along with daily, weekly and monthly trends.

**Flexible and reliable**
- **Timeframe filtering** — view any metric across the last 24 hours, 7 days, 30 days or all time.
- **Continuous tracking** — activity is collected and stored automatically, so your charts stay accurate and persist across restarts.

Clear, actionable insight into exactly how your community is growing and where it is most active.
